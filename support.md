❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
1. How does the AI itinerary generation work?
Wandravel uses advanced AI to generate personalized day-by-day itineraries. It takes into account your selected destination, travel dates, budget (Backpacker, Standard, Luxury), travel style (Chilling, Culture, Adventure), and commute preferences to build a custom schedule just for you.

2. Can I edit an itinerary after it is generated?
Currently, itineraries are fixed once generated. However, if you aren't satisfied with the result, you can easily go back to the "Explore" tab, adjust your preferences, and generate a brand-new itinerary in seconds!

3. Are the prices and transit times accurate?
The prices (in THB) and transit times are AI-generated estimates designed to give you a rough idea of your daily budget and schedule. We recommend verifying specific entry fees and live traffic conditions (e.g., using Google Maps) during your actual trip.

4. Does the app work offline?
No, Wandravel requires an active internet connection to communicate with the AI servers and generate your custom itineraries. However, your previously saved trips in the "My Trips" tab are cached on your device.

5. Why did the AI generate fewer activities than expected?
If you select the "Chilling" travel style, the AI intentionally leaves gaps in your schedule for relaxation. If you prefer a fully packed schedule, try changing your travel style to "Adventure" or "Too Extreme."
