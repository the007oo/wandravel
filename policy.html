# Privacy Policy for Wandravel **Last Updated:** September 17, 2026 Wandravel ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use the Wandravel mobile application. ## 1. Information We Collect - **User Input Data:** We collect the travel preferences you provide (e.g., destinations, dates, budgets, dietary restrictions, and travel styles) to generate personalized itineraries. - **Usage Data & Analytics:** We use Firebase Analytics to collect aggregated usage data (e.g., app interactions, feature usage, and device information) to help us improve the user experience. - **Crash Reports:** We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and diagnostic data to identify and fix technical issues. ## 2. How We Use Your Information - **To Provide the Service:** We send your trip parameters to our AI service (powered by Google Gemini via Firebase AI Logic) to generate customized travel itineraries. - **To Improve Our App:** Analytics and crash data are used strictly for monitoring app stability and improving application features. - **Security & Fraud Prevention:** We use Firebase App Check to ensure that requests to our backend services are legitimate, secure, and originating from unmodified versions of our app. ## 3. Local Storage Your generated itineraries (Day Plans and Activities) are stored locally on your device's internal storage. We do not maintain a central database of your saved trips on our servers. ## 4. Third-Party Services Wandravel utilizes the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies regarding data handling: - **Google Analytics for Firebase** - **Firebase Crashlytics** - **Firebase AI Logic (Google Gemini API)** - **Firebase App Check** Please refer to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for more information on how these services process data. ## 5. Data Security We take reasonable measures to help protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. All communication with our AI backend is secured, and Firebase App Check is integrated to prevent abuse of the backend infrastructure. ## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. ## 7. Contact Us If you have any questions, concerns, or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us.
